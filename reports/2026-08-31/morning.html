5. Concept Unlocked

Labor market / inflation as macro signal → here via the front-end repricing

Long and variable lags- What it is (plain English): When the Fed changes policy, the full effect on inflation and growth doesn't show up for many months — often 12 to 18. Milton Friedman's phrase for the gap between the cause (a rate move) and the visible effect. - The mechanism: Rate changes flow through borrowing, hiring and investment decisions slowly, so the economy you see today reflects policy set over a year ago — which is exactly why a Fed official like Warsh warning about inflation now implies action that won't bite until well into 2027. - Today's live example: The belly repriced +8.5bp on Warsh's words alone — the market is pricing a policy path whose real economic effect is a year away, yet Russell 2000 fell -1.39% today. The asset price moves instantly; the economy moves with a lag. - When this is your edge: When you can separate the immediate repricing (tradeable now) from the delayed real effect (positionable for months), you avoid confusing a rate scare with an actual growth break.
