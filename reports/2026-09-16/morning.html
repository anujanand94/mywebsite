4. Concept Unlocked

Capitalization vs Expensing (via Carney's mega-deduction)- What it is: Normally a company spreads the cost of a machine over years (depreciation); full expensing lets it deduct the whole cost immediately. Same total deduction, very different timing. - The mechanism: Front-loading the deduction cuts this year's taxable income, boosting near-term after-tax cash flow and lifting the internal rate of return on new investment — which pulls capex forward. - Today's live example: Carney's full-expensing plan for machinery and clean energy raises the after-tax return on TSX industrial and energy capex, a multi-year FCF lever that didn't move today's -0.34% tape. - When this is your edge: When policy shifts investment timing before the market prices the cash-flow impact — you buy the re-rating early.
